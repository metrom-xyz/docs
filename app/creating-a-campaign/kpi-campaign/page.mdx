## Creating a campaign with a KPI

Creating a basic campaign on Metrom is straightforward, as covered in
[creating a campaign](/creating-a-campaign). Launching one with an attached KPI
is just as easy. For a quick refresher on KPIs, check out
[KPI based liquidity mining](/kpi).

### Configuration

Once all the required steps are completed, it's time to configure the KPI and
explore the available configuration options.

<div className="mediaWrapper">
  ![Empty KPI step](/images/creation-form-empty-kpi.png)
</div>

- **Current TVL**: Represents the most recent total value locked in the selected
  pool, which is being incentivized.

- **Lower and upper bounds**: These values allow you to customize the
  incentivization strategy by restricting reward distribution to a specific TVL
  range. For inspiration, check out the [KPI usecases](/kpi/usecases).

- **Minimum payout**: This is the percentage of rewards that will be distributed
  to LPs, regardless of the KPI.

### Define your strategy
 
Metrom provides four reward distribution strategies for KPI campaigns. 
Each one shapes how APR behaves as TVL moves inside the bounds you set.
 
#### Traditional reward
 
APR starts high when TVL is low and decreases as TVL grows.
 
Best for bootstrapping liquidity quickly. Early LPs see strong yields, and emissions 
taper as the pool fills up, keeping the cost of acquiring each marginal dollar of liquidity 
in check.

<div className="mediaWrapper">
  ![Traditional reward](/images/kpi-reward-traditional.png)
</div>

#### Capped reward rate
 
APR stays flat up to a specified TVL, then drops past that point.
 
Best for predictable yields up to a known target. Once TVL crosses the upper bound, 
additional liquidity earns less, which discourages overshooting and reduces wasted emissions 
on excess capital.

<div className="mediaWrapper">
  ![Capped reward](/images/kpi-reward-capped.png)
</div>

#### Increasing APR
 
APR rises with TVL and stops paying rewards below a set TVL.
 
Best for protocols that need to hit a minimum scale before incentives kick in. No rewards flow 
until the pool clears the lower bound, then APR climbs as more liquidity is added, rewarding 
LPs more as the pool gets healthier.

<div className="mediaWrapper">
  ![Increasing APR](/images/kpi-reward-increasing.png)
</div>

#### Custom reward rate
 
Use your own curve to shape how rewards are distributed across the TVL range.
 
Best for protocols with specific incentive logic that doesn't fit the three presets. 
You define the distribution behavior end to end.

The simulation chart updates live as you adjust the strategy and parameters. 
It shows two things:

- **The APR curve** in orange, plotted against TVL.
- **The rewards distribution area** in green, showing how much of the total reward pool is paid out at each TVL level.

Here as an example we have set a custom reward curve for a pool with a lower bound of
`$250,000` and a upper bound of `$700,000`, with a 17% minimum payout.

Use the simulation to sanity-check the curve before saving. If it shows 
`MISSING DATA FOR THE SIMULATION`, fill in the bounds and minimum payout so the 
chart can compute the projection.

<div className="mediaWrapper">
  ![Custom reward](/images/kpi-reward-custom.png)
</div>

Once you're satisfied with the KPI setup, save the changes and head to see
campaign preview, where you can review all the details. 

<div className="mediaWrapper">
  ![Preview with KPI](/images/preview-with-kpi.png)
</div>

Connect wallet to transfer reward tokens
and launch the campaign.
