## Creating a campaign

Creating a campaign involves a quick 2 step process - choose the **chain**, **DEX** and **pool**
that you want to run the incentives on, pick the **duration** and then choose the
**rewards**.

### Step 1: Select **chain**, **DEX**, **pool** and **duration** of the campaign

Select the **chain**, **DEX**, **pool** and **duration** for which you want to run the incentives. The time
displayed here is your local time.

<div className="mediaWrapper">
  ![Select DEX and pool](/images/select-dex-pool-duration.png)
</div>

### Step 2: Add reward tokens

Pick the rewards (you can add up to 5 different reward tokens) and its amount
and click on **Next** to save the progress and then finally **Launch campaign**.

<div className="mediaWrapper">![Add rewards](/images/add-rewards.png)</div>

### Campaign preview

Take a quick look at the **campaign preview** on the right side, and then connect wallet to approve
**reward tokens** and **launch campaign**.

<div className="mediaWrapper">![Campaign preview](/images/preview.png)</div>
